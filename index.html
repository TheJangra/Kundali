What must be faced

Ketu in the 7th

Health & Vitality

A Strong Frame Governed by a Sensitive Mind

The 11th-house stellium and a dignified Sun give you genuine vitality and stamina. Your health story is not about the body's strength — it is about protecting the mind and nervous system that steer it, especially through Sade Sati.

Your constitutional map

  • Cancer Lagna & Moon → stomach, chest, and the emotion–digestion axis. Your gut mirrors your mood; feelings you don't process become physical.
  • Moon in Pisces (Revati) → feet, lymph, sleep, and fluid balance. Deep sensitivity that needs rest and water.
  • Rāhu in Lagna + Ashlesha → the nervous system, anxiety, overthinking, occasionally skin. A vivid mind that can run hot.
  • Combust Mars in the 11th → watch inflammation, blood pressure, and burnout from overexertion. Heat needs an outlet.

The Sade Sati factor

With Saturn transiting your Moon (peak 2025–2027), the classic vulnerabilities are sleep, stress, immunity, and low mood. This is not illness — it is the body asking for structure. Saturn heals whatever you meet with routine.

Protective rhythm

Fixed sleep and wake times, daily movement, and unhurried meals will carry you through Sade Sati more than any supplement.

Your vitality prescription

  • Guard the mind first. Daily meditation or pranayama directly settles the Rāhu–Moon nervous energy. Non-negotiable during Sade Sati.
  • Tend the gut. Warm, regular, unhurried meals; limit stimulants when anxious. Your digestion is your emotional barometer.
  • Move the Mars heat. Regular vigorous exercise channels combust Mars into strength instead of inflammation or irritability.
  • Water & feet. Hydration, time near water, and simple foot care soothe the Pisces Moon.
  • Sunrise light. Surya Arghya at dawn resets the circadian clock and strengthens the Sun that rules your immunity and heart.
