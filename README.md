# 🔯 The Celestial Sanctuary — Vedic Kundali Dashboard

An interactive, single-file **Vedic Kundali (birth chart) dashboard** rendered in a
"Celestial Noir" visual style — deep purples, golds, and midnight blues, with an
animated starfield, rotating mandalas, and an SVG North-Indian Lagna chart.

**Birth data analysed:** 29 May 2000 · 11:05 AM · Alwar, Rajasthan (27.57° N, 76.63° E)

## What's inside

- **`index.html`** — the complete dashboard. Self-contained: no build step, no
  external requests, no dependencies. Open it in any modern browser.

### Features
- **North-Indian Lagna chart** drawn as clean SVG (whole-sign houses).
- **Five interactive chambers (tabs):** Overview · Career & Karma · Love & Union ·
  Health & Vitality · Wealth / Artha.
- **The five-pillar analysis:**
  1. *The Foundation* — Ascendant (Cancer / Ashlesha) & Moon (Pisces / Revati),
     including the esoteric soul-purpose of the nakshatra.
  2. *Career & Karma* — 10th house, debilitated Saturn + Jupiter, D10 themes,
     high-growth tech sectors, and the timing of "The Great Switch."
  3. *The Time Stream* — current Vimśottari Mahādaśā/Antardaśā and Sade Sati.
  4. *Abundance & Relationship Alchemy* — 2nd/11th (wealth) and 7th/Venus (love),
     with candid "Hard Truths" and "Great Strengths."
  5. *The Upāyas* — personalised, practical remedies (mantras, gems, behaviour).
- **Celestial Noir** by default, with an opt-in **Celestial Dawn** light theme
  (top-right toggle). Fully keyboard-navigable tabs; respects
  `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Calculation notes

Planetary positions were computed with the **Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) ayanāṁśa**
(~23°51′ for the date) and whole-sign houses. Birth-time-sensitive points — the
Ascendant degree and the Moon's exact minute — should be confirmed against
certified ephemeris software; the signs are robust, but the exact daśā balance is
sensitive to the precise birth time.

*Jyotiṣa is offered here as a lamp for reflection, not a claim of fixed fate.*
